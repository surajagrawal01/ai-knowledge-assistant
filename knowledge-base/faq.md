# ElectroNest Support FAQ

Short answers to common customer questions. Each answer repeats the owning policy's number exactly and cites the section it comes from.

## Returns

### How long do I have to return earbuds?
Audio products such as the NestBuds Pro have a 7-day return window from delivery (Return Policy §2.1).

### Can I return a NestBook after a week?
Yes. Laptops have a 10-day return window from delivery, so a NestBook delivered a week ago can still be returned (Return Policy §2.2).

### Can I return a phone I have already used?
No. A returned item must be unused and in its original box, unless it was damaged on arrival (Return Policy §2.3).

### My phone arrived with a cracked screen. What do I do?
Report it within 48 hours of delivery, with photos of the phone and the packaging (Return Policy §3.1). You can then choose a replacement or a full refund (Return Policy §3.2).

## Refunds

### I returned my phone. When do I get my money back?
A refund is processed within 5–7 business days after the return is picked up (Refund Policy §2).

### I paid by UPI. Where will my refund go?
Refunds for UPI, card and net banking payments go back to the original payment method (Refund Policy §3.1).

### My COD order was returned. How do I get my money?
Refunds for cash on delivery orders are made by bank transfer only. You share your bank account details through the secure form sent by email (Refund Policy §3.3).

### What happens to my EMI if I return a laptop?
The EMI plan is cancelled. Any interest already charged is handled by your bank (Refund Policy §3.2).

### Can I cancel my order?
Yes, free of charge, as long as it has not been shipped yet; the payment is refunded automatically. After shipping, use the return process instead (Refund Policy §5).

## Payments

### Money was deducted but my order failed. What happens now?
For a failed UPI or card payment, your bank automatically reverses the amount within 5–7 business days. Support raises a high-priority payment ticket if you report the debit (Refund Policy §6).

## Shipping

### How long does delivery take to Jaipur?
Jaipur is a tier-2 city in zone B, where delivery takes 4–5 days from the date the order is shipped (Shipping Policy §2.1).

### Do you charge for shipping on a ₹999 order?
Yes. Orders up to ₹999 pay the zone's shipping charge: ₹49 in zone A, ₹79 in zone B and ₹99 in zone C (Shipping Policy §2.1). Shipping is free only on orders above ₹999 (Shipping Policy §2.2).

### My order to Lucknow hasn't arrived after 6 days. Is it late?
Not yet. A zone B delivery counts as delayed only after 7 days from shipping (Shipping Policy §3.1).

## Warranty

### Is a laptop battery problem covered by warranty?
Yes. Laptops are covered for 12 months from delivery, and manufacturing defects in the battery are covered (Warranty Policy §3.1).

### Is a cracked screen covered by warranty?
No. Physical damage such as a cracked screen is not covered (Warranty Policy §4.2).

### My headphones stopped working after 4 months. Are they covered?
Audio products are covered for 6 months from delivery, so a manufacturing defect after 4 months is covered (Warranty Policy §3.1).

### My phone's battery is swelling. What should I do?
Stop using the phone immediately. A swelling battery is a safety issue and is escalated to a human supervisor straight away (Warranty Policy §4.1).

## Support notes

### Do premium customers get different policies?
No. Premium customers have the same policies as every other customer, but their tickets get one priority level higher: low becomes medium, and medium becomes high.
